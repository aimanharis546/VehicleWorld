What Is a Vehicle? 🤔

A vehicle is a machine that carries people or goods from one place to another. Vehicles come in many shapes and sizes, and each type is built for a different kind of journey.

About Vehicle World 🌍

Vehicle World is a learning website that introduces different types of vehicles: road, water, air and rail. Explore our learning pages and discover how vehicles help people every day.

Water Vehicles

✈️

Air Vehicles

Airplanes and helicopters that fly through the sky.

☁️ Fun fact: Airplanes can carry many passengers across long distances!

🚆

Rail Vehicles

Trains that travel along railway tracks.

🚉 Fun fact: Some trains carry hundreds of passengers on one journey!
