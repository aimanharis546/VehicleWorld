What Are Road Vehicles?

Road vehicles help us travel from one place to another. Some carry people, while others help us during emergencies.

Meet the Road Vehicles!

Car

A car takes people from one place to another.

Bus

A bus carries many people at the same time.

Police Car

A police car helps police officers keep us safe.

Ambulance

An ambulance takes sick or injured people to hospital.

Fire Truck

A fire truck helps firefighters put out fires.

Motorcycle

A motorcycle has two wheels and carries people.

⭐ Fun Fact!

Always wear your seat belt when travelling in a car!
