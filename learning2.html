Different Types of Vehicles

Not all vehicles travel on roads. Some travel on water, some fly in the sky, and others move along railway tracks.

🌊 Water Vehicles

Boat

A boat carries people or things across water.

Ship

A ship is a large vehicle that travels across water.

Submarine

A submarine travels underwater.

☁️ Air Vehicles

Airplane

An airplane flies high in the sky and carries passengers.

Helicopter

A helicopter can fly and land in different places.

🚆 Rail Vehicles

Train

A train carries people or goods along railway tracks.

⭐ Remember!

Different vehicles travel in different places: on roads, on water, in the air and on railway tracks!
